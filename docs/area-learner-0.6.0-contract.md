# Area scoring and adaptive minimums: 0.6.0 restoration

This is a fresh implementation of the owner's later request to restore area scoring and acceptance-driven adaptive minimums with Autopilot. It starts from published 0.5.11 and its reviewed release-pipeline follow-up. Available historical source `76b2c338312fbdb13ea7b5ecd0c1df372f02307c` supplies prior behavior; the unavailable `wip/area-060` / `7ff5fc1` branch and its reported tests are not recovered provenance.

## Scope and choices

- Only the current three money axes participate: payout, pay per mile, and pay per minute (displayed per hour). Max stops is an independent hard limit. Retired stop/item/hotspot spokes and the manual percentage drag stay absent.
- Strict is the default; area mode and acceptance learning are independent choices, initially off. Existing saved minima and Autopilot settings survive migration. Fresh learner state is empty; deleted old evidence is not reconstructed from history or retired keys.
- The ratchet learns only from positively confirmed manual standalone acceptance through the current outcome detector. No automatic acceptance, add-on, requested tap or ambiguous outcome teaches it. Unknown numeric facts never teach their axes; independently known eligible axes may still learn. Manual-decline teaching is deliberately excluded from this acceptance-driven restoration.
- Highest accepted payout asks one cent more than that payout. Independent valid observed mile/minute rates retain exact numerator/denominator values and match the best rate. Rate costs remain exact through aggregation and the Autopilot bar, followed by one final cent ceiling. This is the current arithmetic contract, not a claim of binary equivalence to historical early rounding.
- A known implausible figure rejects the entire lesson, including payout: fewer than five minutes, fewer than half a mile, or fewer than two observed stops. These are evidence-quality guards, not restored stop scoring. A mile rate needs at least two observed miles and a minute rate at least ten observed minutes. Missing optional axes stay unset while independently known eligible axes may learn. Existing positive no-tap manual-acceptance evidence remains eligible; no new tap requirement is added.
- Learned values do not change saved knobs. Enabled valid learned floors may activate an unset supported money axis. Disabling or pausing preserves learning but does not gather lessons while off. Reset clears active learning and learner Undo with confirmation; previously recorded decision snapshots remain in local history. Clear history clears those snapshots, learned bests, pending learner evidence and learner Undo; already adopted saved minima remain settings.
- Adopt raises only lower saved minima. Its Undo uses current-state and generation checks, cannot overwrite intervening user or Autopilot edits, and stays distinct from existing Autopilot growth Undo. Retain accessible timeout/focus behavior.
- Current bar-based minimum growth remains separate from acceptance learning. It waits while any enabled learned floor exceeds its saved counterpart, including an otherwise unset axis: growing only saved knobs and resetting the bar must not silently lower effective learned requirements or claim growth when no knob changed. Equality permits the existing behavior. Growth resumes when saved minima cover the learned floors (for example after Adopt), or learning is off. Effective model/learner changes invalidate stale Autopilot plans and frozen optional-study forecasts. They do not disclose learned snapshots in the study payload.

## Scoring and authority

Area remains a compensating standalone filter. Shared resolved floors feed decisions, geometry and explanations. The normalized polygon uses current supported axis positions and the square-root area fitness; do not quietly replace it with a strict maximum or rearrange geometry. With requirements a, b and c in current axis order, the exact squared requirement is 2abc/(a+c). One axis uses a squared; two axes use ab, explicitly including the opposite payout/minute pair whose literal polygon is degenerate. Multiple active axes with a known zero requirement give a zero-route/degenerate REVIEW, not an unread-fact claim or infinite reciprocal compensation. A lone known-zero axis asks zero and has no score. Unknown active facts remain REVIEW unless a separate authorized hard failure such as max stops already determines the result. In incomplete area mode, one known individual money ask failing is not such a failure: another unread axis might compensate. Threshold, Autopilot and growth calculations must preserve these distinctions.

A distinguishing fixture uses payout 800 cents, 10 miles and 20 minutes against saved minima 500 cents, 100 cents/mile and 25 cents/minute. Its asks are 500/1000/500. At the current axis angles the area requirement is approximately 707.107 cents: area permits 800 at bar 100, while strict declines it. Bar 120 requires 849 cents after final rounding. Exact threshold tests must not decide from a rounded display score.

Within a fixed active-axis set, increasing fixed/learned floors or the bar cannot loosen a decision; lowering max stops cannot turn a decline into a permit. Learned-axis activation needs its own regression and explicit topology treatment rather than an unsupported global monotonic claim across changing active sets.

Learning affects standalone offers only. Add-ons stay strict against the saved minima and current Autopilot bar, without learned floors; adopting raised saved minima therefore also strengthens add-on checks. Optional automatic acceptance stays off by default, separately confirmed, and subject to every current dispatch guard. A standalone automatic acceptance must meet each resolved saved and enabled learned money minimum at 100% or a higher Autopilot bar even while the standalone filter uses area mode. Merely switching mode grants no additional automatic action authority. No automatic outcome or add-on trains the learner. On-demand explanations must distinguish all three boundaries.

## Presentation, privacy and validation

Expose separate mode, learner and Autopilot controls without reusing their identities. Distinguish saved, learned and bar-adjusted requirements in the graph, ticket, history and reports. A stored new best, an effective increase and no change are different outcomes. Recorded history keeps its original model/score; current-rule projections must be labeled as current. Preserve short-window facts, primary touch targets, large text and all shipped 0.5.11 navigation/report behavior.

Consent 17 describes the new bounded numeric local learning while retaining the separately saved study choice and withdrawal behavior. Learner persistence includes no customer text, screenshots or coordinates. Decision-time saved/learned snapshots remain local, including snapshots folded into notification history; outgoing offer reports and their remasking remove these snapshots. Summary and study paths do not append learned bests. Previously authorized user-selected diagnostic facts and current saved rules keep their existing boundaries. Disable, Reset, Clear history and consent revocation invalidate pending writes; replay and duplicate outcomes cannot retrain or resurrect cleared state.

The exact historical “Permit” wording was not recovered. Any new wording must accurately distinguish a filter result from an observed acceptance. Stop spacing remains held: distance divided by stops is not observed ordered-leg geometry. Supported map-favoring split sizing and physical-device results remain separate open requirements; neither a universal divider API nor a phone PASS is invented here.

Focused local tests cover the fresh implementation. The canonical final dual-SDK/lint/build/sign, acceptance, publication and live-update gate runs only on the actual final remote source commit. Historical reports, source checks and phone results remain distinct. This document is a contract, not a release or handset receipt.
