## Existing pilot sequence remains binding

This supplements the historical pilot policy, retained here as the sanitized public `OfferFilter_Controlled_Pilot-v3.md`. Its original status rows predate signing and the frozen candidate; the build table above refreshes those facts without replacing the cohort design.

- Use one shared app, release and automatic-update channel for everyone. There is no pilot-only APK, private feed, invitation/license gate or withholding of fixes.
- Plan ten active adult volunteer slots over seven days, admitting three first and at most fifteen total enrollments including replacements. The pilot selects Android 11 or newer; the app's Android 8/API 26 support floor is unchanged. Aim for two manufacturers among the first three.
- Review every first-three stationary checklist, **one ordinary voluntary session each**, private reports and incident status before admitting the other seven. Silence or a download does not satisfy this gate. Keep Autopilot, auto-accept and Peek off in the initial cohort.
- Target two ordinary sessions per participant, approximately twenty total, without work-hour requirements, induced orders or decline quotas. Separately count enrollment, self-reported installation, active sessions and received usable study data.
- Agree a first-session check-in normally within 48 hours. At most one permitted follow-up and one reschedule; after a further 24 hours without reply, an opted-in replacement may take the supported seat within the enrollment cap. Never disable the absent person's app or infer non-use from silence. No follow-up is authorized or scheduled by this packet.
- The study choice may start checked, but recording requires affirmative Save choice. General notice acceptance is not study consent; automatic after-dash reporting remains a separate choice. Withdrawal preserves app usability and removes study content from unsent reports; it cannot retract an already in-flight or stored request.
- Fewer than five participants with actually received, reviewed usable study records means the prediction part is insufficient. Even above that practical target, report coverage and right-censoring first; completed-only errors are conditional and potentially biased. Freeze estimate/time/method before the outcome, keep builds and policy epochs separate, and never relabel historical fitted means as forecasts. - A critical incident stops active testing/admissions for owner triage; a blocking install/consent/reporting problem holds the affected build/study.
